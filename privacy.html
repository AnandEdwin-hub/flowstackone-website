Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

flowstackONE ("we", "us", "our") operates the InkDesk platform and related services available at flowstackone.com and app.flowstackone.com.

We build AI-powered receptionist tools for small and medium businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our services.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: hello@flowstackone.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — name, email address, phone number, and business details when you sign up or contact us
  • Customer inquiry data — messages, DMs, and call transcripts that customers send to businesses using InkDesk
  • Usage data — how you interact with our dashboard and services
  • Automatically collected data — IP address, browser type, and device information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the InkDesk platform and AI receptionist services
  • Process and qualify customer inquiries on behalf of businesses using our platform
  • Send service updates, summaries, and notifications related to your account
  • Respond to support requests and communications
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Messaging and Social Media Integrations

InkDesk integrates with messaging platforms including Instagram, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp to help businesses respond to customer inquiries automatically.

When a customer sends a message to a business using InkDesk through these platforms:

  • The message content is processed by our AI to generate a response
  • Customer name, contact details, and inquiry details may be stored to create a lead record for the business
  • Conversation history is stored securely and accessible only to the business owner
  • We do not use customer messages for advertising purposes

Our use of data received from Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp) complies with Meta's Platform Terms and Data Policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a GDPR-compliant database platform. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Anthropic / OpenRouter — AI language model for conversation processing
  • Meta (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp) — messaging platform integrations
  • Render — cloud infrastructure for our backend services
  • Vercel — hosting for our web applications

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and data handling practices.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@flowstackone.com.

8. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for the site to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
